2. LEAVE AND TIME OFF

2.1 ANNUAL LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 26 working days of annual leave per year.
Leave is accrued monthly at a rate of 2.17 days from the start date.
A maximum of 9 unused leave days may be carried over to the following year.
Any unused leave beyond 9 days is forfeited. There is no cash payout except upon resignation.

2.2 HOW TO REQUEST ANNUAL LEAVE
1. Log into the HRMS system at hr.arianatech.ir.
2. Select "Leave Request".
3. Choose the start and end dates.
4. Obtain approval from your direct supervisor (SLA: 48 hours).
5. HR approval is required for leave longer than 5 days.

2.3 LEAVE REQUEST DEADLINES
Leave up to 3 days: at least 2 working days in advance.
Leave from 4 to 10 days: at least 1 week in advance.
Leave longer than 10 days: at least 1 month in advance.

2.4 SICK LEAVE
Maximum of 10 days per year with a doctor's confirmation.
Up to 3 days: no medical certificate required (self-declared).
More than 3 days: a certificate from an approved doctor is mandatory.
More than 10 days: referred to Social Security insurance.

2.5 MATERNITY LEAVE
9 months (270 days) in accordance with Iranian Labor Law.
Payment is made through Social Security insurance.
May be extended using annual leave.

2.6 PATERNITY LEAVE
3 days with full pay.
Must be used within 1 month of the child's birth.

2.7 MARRIAGE LEAVE
3 days with full pay.
Available once during employment.

2.8 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
First-degree relatives (parents, spouse, children, siblings): 3 days.
Second-degree relatives: 1 day.

2.9 UNPAID LEAVE
Maximum of 1 month per year.
Requires approval from the department manager and HR.
No salary is paid and no insurance record is accrued.

2.10 EMERGENCY LEAVE
Up to 2 days per incident.
Approved verbally by the supervisor and registered in the system afterwards.

2.11 PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
According to the official calendar of the Islamic Republic of Iran.
Religious and national holidays are observed.
The annual holiday calendar is announced at the beginning of each year.

2.12 LEAVE DURING PROBATION
During the first 3 months: 2 days of annual leave.
Sick leave follows the normal procedure.

2.13 LEAVE FOR PART-TIME EMPLOYEES
Calculated in proportion to working hours.
Example: A part-time employee (20 hours per week) is entitled to 13 days per year.
