10. REMOTE WORK POLICY

10.1 GENERAL POLICY
Hybrid model: 2 remote days plus 3 on-site days (Saturday to Wednesday).
Thursday: remote for everyone.
Requires supervisor approval.

10.2 ELIGIBILITY
At least 6 months of service.
Acceptable performance in the last evaluation.
Role suitable for remote work (supervisor approval).
Stable internet at the residence.

10.3 REMOTE DAYS
Days selected in coordination with the team.
At least 2 on-site days per week are mandatory.
Mandatory on-site days: team meetings, training sessions.

10.4 REMOTE WORK FROM ANOTHER CITY
Requires senior manager approval.
Maximum of 1 month per year.
No change in salary or benefits.

10.5 REMOTE WORK FROM ABROAD
Prohibited due to legal and security concerns.
Exception: with CEO approval.

10.6 REMOTE WORK EQUIPMENT
Provided by the company: laptop, headset, mouse.
Provided by the employee: desk, chair, internet.
Internet allowance: 10 percent of monthly salary.

10.7 EXPECTATIONS
Availability during working hours (08:30 to 17:30).
Participation in online meetings.
Attendance recorded in the app.
Daily report to the supervisor (optional).

10.8 PERFORMANCE EVALUATION FOR REMOTE WORK
Based on output, not presence hours.
Monthly evaluation by the supervisor.
Failure to meet goals: return to full on-site work.

10.9 FLEXIBLE HOURS
Arrival: 08:00 to 10:00.
Departure: 8 hours after arrival.
Core Hours: 10:00 to 15:00.
Team coordination required.

10.10 PART-TIME WORK
Available for certain roles.
Minimum of 20 hours per week.
Benefits proportional to hours.

10.11 JOB SHARING
Two employees share one role.
Requires manager and HR approval.
Benefits proportional to hours.
