8. CODE OF CONDUCT

8.1 GENERAL PRINCIPLES
Mutual respect in all interactions.
Honesty and transparency.
Respect for the privacy of colleagues.
Avoidance of discrimination.
Compliance with the laws of the Islamic Republic of Iran.

8.2 CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Disclose any conflict of interest to HR.
Avoid participating in decisions involving relatives.
First-degree relatives may not be hired in the same team without senior management approval.

8.3 GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY
Accepting gifts up to 2 million rials is permitted.
Gifts above the limit require manager approval.
Cash gifts are prohibited.
Receiving gifts from suppliers during procurement is prohibited.

8.4 ANTI-BRIBERY AND ANTI-CORRUPTION
Any form of bribery is prohibited and leads to termination of employment.
Report corruption through the confidential channel ethics@arianatech.ir.
Whistleblower protection is provided.

8.5 HARASSMENT
Any form of sexual, verbal, or psychological harassment is prohibited.
Report to hr@arianatech.ir or ethics@arianatech.ir.
Investigations are confidential and prompt (within 14 days).
Penalty: up to termination of employment.

8.6 DISCRIMINATION
Discrimination based on gender, ethnicity, religion, age, or disability is prohibited.
Equal opportunities for all.
Complaints to HR.

8.7 SUBSTANCE ABUSE
Use of drugs in the workplace is strictly prohibited.
Alcohol consumption is prohibited.
If observed, report to HR.
Support is provided for employees in rehabilitation (optional).

8.8 WORKPLACE VIOLENCE
Any threat, assault, or violent behavior is prohibited.
Leads to immediate termination and legal action.

8.9 DRESS CODE
Formal business attire on client-facing days.
Business casual on regular days.
Compliance with Islamic norms and organizational customs.

8.10 SOCIAL MEDIA
Publishing confidential company information is prohibited.
Publishing personal opinions on behalf of the company is prohibited.
Social media use during working hours is limited.
The code of conduct applies in online spaces.

8.11 CONFIDENTIALITY
NDA signed upon hiring.
Confidential information must not be disclosed for 5 years after departure.
Confidential information includes: code, customer data, strategy, financials.

8.12 INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work created during employment is the property of the company.
Patents are registered in the name of the employee and the company.
Use of unlicensed software is prohibited.

8.13 REPORTING VIOLATIONS
Reporting channels:
- ethics@arianatech.ir
- Confidential hotline: 021-12345678, extension 999
- Suggestion boxes on each floor
Reports are investigated confidentially.
Retaliation against the reporter is a serious violation.
