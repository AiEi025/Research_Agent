9. HEALTH, SAFETY AND WELLBEING

9.1 HEALTH AND SAFETY POLICY
Commitment to providing a safe work environment.
Periodic safety training.
Regular workplace inspections.

9.2 INCIDENT REPORTING
Every incident must be reported within 24 hours.
Incident report form available in HRMS.
Follow-up by the safety committee.
Work-related incidents: covered by Social Security insurance.

9.3 FIRST AID
First aid kit on each floor.
Trained first-aiders in each team.
Emergency number: 115.

9.4 WELLNESS PROGRAM
Gym: 20 percent discount, partnership with 10 gyms.
Yoga and meditation: free weekly class.
Psychological counseling: 4 free sessions per year.
Annual checkup: free for employees over 40.

9.5 MENTAL HEALTH
Employee Assistance Program (EAP).
24-hour counseling line: 021-12345679.
In-person counseling sessions (by appointment).
Full confidentiality.

9.6 ERGONOMICS
Standard chair and desk.
Adjustable monitor.
Short breaks every 2 hours.
Ergonomic equipment requests through IT.

9.7 EMERGENCY
Marked emergency exit routes.
Drills every 6 months.
Safety officer on each floor.
Fire extinguishers at designated points.

9.8 SMOKING
Prohibited inside the building.
Designated smoking area in the courtyard.
Support program to quit smoking.

9.9 ALCOHOL AND DRUGS
Prohibited in the workplace and at official events.
Violation: referred to HR.

9.10 WORK-LIFE BALANCE
Respect for non-working hours.
No work calls after 19:00 (except emergencies).
Fridays: no meetings.
Holidays: fully respected.
