5. PERFORMANCE AND CAREER

5.1 EVALUATION CYCLE
Quarterly: review of OKR and KPI progress.
Semi-annual: mid-year review.
Annual: comprehensive evaluation and salary review.

5.2 EVALUATION CRITERIA
Achievement of goals (OKR/KPI): 40 percent.
Quality of work: 20 percent.
Teamwork: 15 percent.
Innovation and initiative: 15 percent.
Personal development: 10 percent.

5.3 PERFORMANCE LEVELS
Exceeds: 5 - eligible for promotion and maximum bonus.
Meets: 4 - standard bonus.
Partially Meets: 3 - needs improvement.
Below: 2 - Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
Unacceptable: 1 - possible termination.

5.4 PROMOTION PROCESS
1. Annual evaluation with a score of 4 or above.
2. At least 18 months in the current level.
3. Approval from the supervisor and department manager.
4. Interview with the promotion committee.
5. Final approval by HR.

5.5 CAREER PATHS
Technical: Junior to Mid to Senior to Lead to Principal to Architect.
Managerial: Team Lead to Manager to Director to VP to C-Level.
Specialist: Specialist to Senior Specialist to Expert to Consultant.

5.6 PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN (PIP)
Duration: 90 days.
Specific and measurable goals.
Weekly meetings with the supervisor.
Final evaluation: if no improvement, employment is terminated.

5.7 INTERNAL MOBILITY
At least 12 months in the current position.
Notify the current supervisor.
Apply through HRMS.
Internal interview process.

5.8 FAST TRACK PROMOTION
For exceptional and outstanding performance.
Requires senior management approval.
Minimum 12 months instead of 18 months.
