6. ONBOARDING AND PROBATION

6.1 REQUIRED DOCUMENTS ON THE FIRST DAY
Original and copy of national ID card.
Original and copy of birth certificate.
Original and copy of the highest academic degree.
Four passport-size photos.
Bank account number.
Certificate of no criminal record (for sensitive roles).
Completed personal information form.
National ID number and postal code.

6.2 ONBOARDING PROCESS
Day one: desk setup, laptop and access card delivery, team introduction, HR orientation session.
First week: internal systems training, signing of contract and confidentiality forms, participation in team meetings, introduction to products and clients.
First month: completion of mandatory training courses, assignment of a mentor, feedback session with the manager.

6.3 PROBATION PERIOD
Duration: 3 months.
May be extended up to 6 months (by mutual agreement).
Salary: 100 percent from the start.
Benefits: full, except for certain items.

6.4 PROBATION EVALUATION
Mid-term meeting: end of the second month.
Final meeting: end of the third month.
Criteria: technical fit, cultural fit, performance.

6.5 PROBATION OUTCOME
Confirmed: converted to a permanent contract.
Extended: up to another 3 months (only once).
Not confirmed: termination of employment in accordance with the law.

6.6 IMPORTANT NOTES
During probation, either party may terminate the employment with 2 weeks' notice.
Leave: 2 days during a 3-month probation.
Failure to attend mandatory sessions: negative impact on evaluation.
