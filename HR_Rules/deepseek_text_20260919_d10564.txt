15. EMPLOYEE RELATIONS

15.1 RAISING A GRIEVANCE
Submit grievances to HR or the direct manager.
Written grievances preferred.
Confidential handling.
Response within 7 working days.

15.2 GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
Step 1: Informal discussion with the supervisor.
Step 2: Written grievance to HR.
Step 3: Investigation by HR.
Step 4: Decision and communication.
Step 5: Appeal to senior management if needed.

15.3 COMPLAINT HANDLING
All complaints are documented.
Investigations are confidential.
Both parties are heard.
Decision within 14 days.

15.4 FEEDBACK CHANNELS
Suggestion boxes on each floor.
Anonymous feedback form in HRMS.
Quarterly employee engagement survey.
Open-door policy with managers.

15.5 TEAM MEETINGS
Weekly team meetings.
Monthly all-hands meeting.
Quarterly department reviews.

15.6 COMMUNICATION OF POLICY CHANGES
Announced via email and HRMS.
Posted on the intranet.
Effective date clearly stated.
Q&A session if needed.

15.7 HR REPRESENTATIVE
Each department has a dedicated HR business partner.
Contact list available on the intranet.
Available for confidential discussions.

15.8 OPEN-DOOR POLICY
Managers maintain an open-door policy.
Employees may approach any manager.
No retaliation for raising concerns.

15.9 DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Between employees: mediated by HR.
Between employee and manager: escalated to HR and senior management.
External mediation or legal action as a last resort.

15.10 EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT
Annual engagement survey.
Action plans based on results.
Recognition programs.
Team-building activities.
