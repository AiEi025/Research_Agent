4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

4.1 SALARY PAYMENT
Payment date: end of each Iranian calendar month.
Method: direct deposit to the employee's declared bank account.
Payslip: available in HRMS up to 3 days before payment.

4.2 SALARY STRUCTURE
Base salary.
Housing allowance.
Food and grocery allowance.
Child allowance (per child).
Transportation allowance.
Overtime (if applicable).
Performance bonus (quarterly or annual).

4.3 SALARY REVIEW
Conducted annually in April (Farvardin).
Based on: performance, inflation, company budget.
Average annual increase: 15 to 30 percent depending on performance.
Notification: in March of the previous year.

4.4 PERFORMANCE BONUS
Quarterly: up to 30 percent of monthly salary.
Annual: up to 2 times monthly salary (based on evaluation).
Project bonus: depending on the project and agreement.

4.5 INSURANCE
Social Security: full coverage per Labor Law. Employee contribution 7 percent; employer contribution 23 percent.
Supplementary Insurance: provided by Dana or equivalent. Coverage includes hospitalization, paramedical, medication, and dental. Annual cap: 500 million rials. Available for employee, spouse, and children.
Life and Accident Insurance: coverage of 10 times the monthly salary. Includes work-related and non-work-related accidents.

4.6 ALLOWANCES
Meals: free lunch service at headquarters.
Transportation: 15 percent of monthly salary or company shuttle service.
Internet: 10 percent of monthly salary for remote workers.
Sports: 20 percent discount at selected gyms.
Mobile phone: up to 5 million rials per month for relevant roles.

4.7 SALARY ADVANCE
Maximum: 2 times the monthly salary.
Eligibility: at least 6 months of service.
Repayment: up to 6 installments from monthly salary.
Request: through HRMS with HR approval.

4.8 LOANS
Emergency loan: up to 50 million tomans, repaid over 12 months.
Housing loan: through the employee fund (requires 2 years of service).
Marriage loan: up to 30 million tomans.

4.9 SPECIAL BONUSES
Retention bonus: every 3 years of service, one extra month's salary.
Referral bonus: 10 million tomans for each successful hire.
Innovation bonus: up to 20 million tomans for implemented ideas.
