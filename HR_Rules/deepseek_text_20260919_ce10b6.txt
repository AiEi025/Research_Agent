14. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

14.1 TRAINING PROGRAMS
Onboarding training for new hires.
Technical training by role.
Soft skills training (communication, leadership).
Compliance and security training (mandatory).
Language courses (English).

14.2 MANDATORY TRAINING
Information security (annual).
Code of conduct (annual).
Health and safety (annual).
Anti-harassment (annual).
Failure to complete: impact on evaluation.

14.3 HOW TO ENROLL
Through the LMS at lms.arianatech.ir.
Or request through HR.
Approval from supervisor for external courses.

14.4 TUITION REIMBURSEMENT
Up to 50 million rials per year for job-related courses.
Requires prior approval.
Minimum grade of B or equivalent.
Commitment to stay 1 year after completion.

14.5 LEARNING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
LMS at lms.arianatech.ir.
Access to internal and external courses.
Progress tracking.
Certificates upon completion.

14.6 LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT
For managers and high-potential employees.
Mentorship program.
Executive coaching.
Leadership workshops.

14.7 EXTERNAL CONFERENCES
Attendance with supervisor approval.
Company covers registration, travel, and accommodation.
Knowledge sharing session required after the event.

14.8 PROFESSIONAL CERTIFICATIONS
Company supports relevant certifications.
Exam fees reimbursed upon successful completion.
Examples: AWS, PMP, Scrum, CISSP.

14.9 TRAINING BUDGET
Each department has an annual training budget.
Unused budget does not carry over.
Requests through the department manager.

14.10 MENTORSHIP
Every new employee is assigned a mentor for 3 months.
Mentors receive a small stipend.
Mentorship is voluntary.
