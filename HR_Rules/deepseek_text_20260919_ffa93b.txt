12. TRAVEL AND EXPENSES

12.1 GENERAL POLICY
Business travel requires prior approval from the supervisor and manager.
Booking through the administration department.
Use of economy-class transportation.

12.2 TRIP BOOKING
Request in HRMS at least 7 days in advance.
Flight or train booking by the administration department.
Hotel: maximum 4 stars.

12.3 TRAVEL CLASS
Domestic flights: Economy.
International flights under 4 hours: Economy.
International flights over 4 hours: Business (with manager approval).

12.4 PER DIEM
Domestic trips: 5 million rials per day.
International trips: 50 USD per day.
Covers meals and incidentals.
Paid with the next salary.

12.5 HOTEL
Domestic: up to 4 million rials per night.
International: up to 100 USD per night.
Higher rates require prior approval.

12.6 EXPENSE REPORT
Submit within 10 working days after the trip.
Attach all original receipts.
Submit through HRMS.
Approval by supervisor and finance.

12.7 REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
Transportation (taxi, flight, train).
Accommodation.
Meals within per diem.
Business-related phone calls.
Visa and passport fees for business travel.

12.8 NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
Personal entertainment.
Alcoholic beverages.
Traffic fines.
Personal shopping.
Upgrades beyond policy.

12.9 REIMBURSEMENT TIMELINE
Reimbursement is processed within 15 working days after approval.

12.10 TRAVEL APPROVAL
Domestic travel: supervisor approval.
International travel: manager and HR approval.
Travel over 7 days: senior management approval.
