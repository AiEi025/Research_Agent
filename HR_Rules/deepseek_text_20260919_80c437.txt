16. PAYROLL AND DOCUMENTATION

16.1 PAYROLL CUT-OFF DATE
Payroll is calculated up to the 25th of each month.
After the cut-off, changes apply to the next month.

16.2 PAYSLIP ACCESS
Payslips are available in HRMS.
Accessible 3 days before payment.
Historical payslips available for 5 years.

16.3 SALARY DEDUCTIONS
Income tax (per Iranian tax law).
Social Security contribution (7 percent).
Loan installments (if applicable).
Unpaid leave deductions.
Absence deductions.

16.4 TAX CALCULATION
Calculated according to the latest Iranian tax brackets.
Updated annually.
Detailed in each payslip.

16.5 UPDATING BANK DETAILS
Through HRMS.
Requires HR approval.
Effective from the next payroll cycle.

16.6 UPDATING PERSONAL INFORMATION
Through HRMS.
Changes to marital status, address, dependents.
Supporting documents may be required.

16.7 EMPLOYMENT LETTER
Request through HRMS.
Purpose: visa, bank, embassy, etc.
Delivered within 5 working days.
May be in Persian or English.

16.8 SALARY CERTIFICATE
Request through HRMS.
Purpose: loan, visa, rental.
Delivered within 5 working days.
Signed and stamped by HR.

16.9 VISA DOCUMENTS
HR provides: employment letter, salary certificate, tax records.
Employee provides: personal documents.
Processing time: up to 10 working days.

16.10 WORK PERMIT RENEWAL
HR handles renewals for foreign employees.
Employee provides required documents 2 months before expiry.
Company covers government fees.

16.11 EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT
Issued in two copies.
Signed by employee and HR.
Contains: position, salary, benefits, terms.
Modifications require written addendum.
