18. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How many annual leave days do I get?
A: 26 working days per year, accrued monthly at 2.17 days.

Q: Can I carry over unused leave?
A: Yes, up to 9 days to the next year.

Q: How do I request leave?
A: Through the HRMS system at hr.arianatech.ir, with supervisor approval.

Q: What are the working hours?
A: Saturday to Wednesday 08:30 to 17:30, Thursday 08:30 to 13:00. Flexible arrival between 08:00 and 10:00.

Q: Can I work remotely?
A: Yes, hybrid model with 2 remote days per week, subject to supervisor approval and 6 months of service.

Q: When is salary paid?
A: At the end of each Iranian calendar month, by direct deposit.

Q: When is salary reviewed?
A: Annually in April (Farvardin).

Q: What insurance do I get?
A: Social Security, supplementary insurance (Dana), and life and accident insurance.

Q: How long is the probation period?
A: 3 months, extendable up to 6 months.

Q: What is the notice period for resignation?
A: 1 month for regular employees, 3 months for managers.

Q: How do I report harassment?
A: Contact hr@arianatech.ir or ethics@arianatech.ir. Reports are confidential.

Q: Is there a gym discount?
A: Yes, 20 percent discount at 10 partner gyms.

Q: Can I get a salary advance?
A: Yes, up to 2 times the monthly salary after 6 months of service.

Q: How do I request a laptop?
A: Submit a ticket at support.arianatech.ir. SLA is 3 working days.

Q: What is the per diem for domestic travel?
A: 5 million rials per day.

Q: How many sick leave days do I get?
A: Up to 10 days per year with a doctor's confirmation.

Q: How long is maternity leave?
A: 9 months (270 days) per Iranian Labor Law.

Q: Can I work from abroad?
A: No, except with CEO approval.

Q: How do I get an employment letter?
A: Request through HRMS. Delivered within 5 working days.

Q: What is the referral bonus?
A: 10 million tomans for each successful hire.
