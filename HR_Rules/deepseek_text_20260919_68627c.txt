7. RESIGNATION AND TERMINATION

7.1 RESIGNATION
Notice period for regular employees: 1 month.
Notice period for managers and supervisors: 3 months.
Notice period for sensitive roles: per contract, up to 6 months.
Resignation process:
1. Submit a written request to the supervisor and HR.
2. Meeting with HR.
3. Determination of exit date.
4. Handover of projects and documents.
5. Final settlement.

7.2 FAILURE TO SERVE NOTICE
Salary is deducted for the remaining days.
Impact on the end-of-service certificate.
In specific cases: legal action.

7.3 FINAL SETTLEMENT
Payment of salary up to the last working day.
Payment of unused leave (up to 9 days).
Payment of accrued bonuses.
Deduction of remaining loans.
Payment deadline: up to 30 days after the last working day.

7.4 RETURN OF COMPANY PROPERTY
Laptop, mobile phone, access card.
Documents and files.
System access is deactivated.
If not returned: deducted from the final settlement.

7.5 TERMINATION BY THE COMPANY
Valid reasons: poor performance, misconduct, organizational restructuring.
Steps: written warning, PIP, final decision.
Payment: in accordance with Labor Law (severance and end-of-service benefits).

7.6 SEVERANCE AND END-OF-SERVICE
Severance: 1 month's salary per year of service.
End-of-service bonus: per Labor Law.
Unemployment insurance: for eligible employees.

7.7 EXIT INTERVIEW
Optional but recommended.
Conducted by HR.
Confidential feedback.
Goal: improvement of organizational processes.

7.8 EMPLOYMENT CERTIFICATE
Issued upon request.
Includes: start date, end date, position.
Delivered within 5 working days of the request.

7.9 REHIRE
Possible after 6 months from departure.
Requires a new interview.
Previous service is counted for severance purposes.
