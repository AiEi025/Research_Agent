13. DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION

13.1 POLICY STATEMENT
ArianaTech is committed to diversity, equity, and inclusion.
Equal opportunities regardless of gender, ethnicity, religion, age, or disability.

13.2 EQUAL OPPORTUNITY
All hiring, promotion, and compensation decisions are based on merit.
No discrimination in any HR process.

13.3 RELIGIOUS ACCOMMODATION
Flexible hours for religious obligations.
Prayer rooms available on each floor.
Religious holidays observed.

13.4 DISABILITY ACCOMMODATION
Workplace accessibility.
Assistive equipment upon request.
Flexible arrangements where possible.
Requests through HR.

13.5 GENDER EQUALITY
Equal pay for equal work.
Maternity and paternity leave for all eligible employees.
Support for women in leadership.

13.6 EMPLOYEE RESOURCE GROUPS (ERGs)
Women in Tech.
Young Professionals Network.
Wellness and Sports Club.
New ERGs can be proposed to HR.

13.7 DISCRIMINATION COMPLAINTS
Report to hr@arianatech.ir or ethics@arianatech.ir.
Confidential investigation within 14 days.
Protection against retaliation.

13.8 PAY EQUITY
Annual pay equity review.
Adjustments made where discrepancies are found.
Transparency in salary bands.

13.9 INCLUSIVE LANGUAGE
Use inclusive language in all communications.
Respect preferred names and pronouns.
Avoid stereotypes.
