17. GENERAL POLICIES

17.1 LOCATION OF POLICIES
All policies are available on the intranet at intranet.arianatech.ir.
The HRMS also hosts HR-related policies.

17.2 POLICY UPDATES
Reviewed annually or as needed.
Announced via email and intranet.
Effective from the stated date.

17.3 POLICY APPROVAL
HR policies: approved by HR Manager and CEO.
IT policies: approved by IT Manager and CTO.
Financial policies: approved by CFO.

17.4 PETS IN THE WORKPLACE
Pets are not allowed in the workplace.
Exception: service animals with prior approval.

17.5 VISITORS
All visitors must register at reception.
Visitor badges required.
Host must escort visitors.
No unaccompanied visitors in work areas.

17.6 PARKING
Parking spaces assigned based on seniority and role.
Requests through the administration department.
Monthly fee applies for non-assigned spaces.

17.7 OFFICE ACCESS
Access card required at all times.
Access hours: 06:00 to 22:00.
After-hours access: requires supervisor approval.
Lost card: report immediately to security.

17.8 AFTER-HOURS WORK
Requires supervisor approval.
Security notified in advance.
No lone working in restricted areas.

17.9 SECOND JOBS AND FREELANCING
Requires disclosure to HR.
Conflicts of interest must be avoided.
No use of company resources for personal work.
Freelancing in the same industry: prohibited.

17.10 POLITICAL ACTIVITIES
Political activities are not permitted in the workplace.
No distribution of political materials.
Respect for diverse opinions.

17.11 ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY
Recycling bins on each floor.
Paperless where possible.
Energy conservation encouraged.
Sustainable procurement.

17.12 COMPANY EVENTS
Attendance is encouraged.
Professional conduct required.
Alcohol is prohibited.
Transportation provided for late events.

17.13 EMPLOYEE DISCOUNTS
Partnerships with local businesses.
List available on the intranet.
Company ID required.

17.14 LOST AND FOUND
Items collected at reception.
Unclaimed items donated after 30 days.
Report lost items to security.

17.15 SMOKING AREAS
Designated areas only.
Outside main entrances.
No smoking in company vehicles.
