3. WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

3.1 OFFICIAL WORKING HOURS
Saturday to Wednesday: 08:30 to 17:30
Thursday: 08:30 to 13:00
Total: 44 hours per week.

3.2 FLEXIBLE WORKING HOURS
Arrival: between 08:00 and 10:00.
Departure: 8 hours after arrival.
Core Hours: 10:00 to 15:00 (mandatory attendance).
Attendance is recorded via card and mobile app.

3.3 ATTENDANCE RECORDING
Entry and exit via electronic card or mobile app.
Forgotten check-in: up to 3 times per month with supervisor's notice.
More than 3 times: deducted from annual leave.

3.4 LATENESS
Grace period: 15 minutes.
Lateness beyond 15 minutes: deducted from leave.
Repeated lateness (more than 5 times per month): written warning.

3.5 OVERTIME
Requires prior approval from the supervisor.
Rate: 1.4 times the normal hourly rate (per Labor Law).
Overtime on public holidays: 2 times the normal rate.
Maximum: 40 hours per month.
Payment: with next month's salary or converted to leave (employee's choice).

3.6 WORKING ON HOLIDAYS
Requires approval from the department manager.
Paid at 2 times the normal rate or 2 times compensatory leave.

3.7 ABSENCE
Unexcused absence: 1.5 days deducted from leave per day of absence.
More than 3 consecutive days of unexcused absence: referred to HR.
Repeated absence: may lead to termination of employment.
